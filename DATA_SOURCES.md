# Data Sources and Safety Notes

SafeMap presents domestic-violence awareness information and support resources. The topic is sensitive, so data and wording should be handled conservatively.

## Data Principles

- Prefer WHO, UN Women, World Bank, national statistics offices, and established local support organisations.
- Treat country-level figures as directional indicators, not case-level truth.
- Show source context wherever possible: source name, year, geography, and known caveats.
- Avoid ranking language that could shame countries or communities.

## Resource Validation

- Check support-service links at least quarterly.
- Prefer official helplines, government pages, or established charities.
- Keep quick-exit behaviour visible and keyboard-accessible.
- Avoid storing personal data or search history.

## Content Disclaimer

SafeMap is an awareness dashboard, not emergency advice. Anyone in immediate danger should contact local emergency services or a trusted local support organisation.
